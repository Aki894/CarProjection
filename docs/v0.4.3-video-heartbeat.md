# 0.4.3: independent CarLife video-channel heartbeat

## Evidence from the 2026-10-02 physical tests

Both 0.4.2 logs use direct CarPlay video. Neither contains a new USB read/write exception or video queue overflow. Both close through `HU_SILENT_TIMEOUT`; this is the phone's subsequent detection, not an explanation of why the HU stopped communicating.

In the 22:48:18 export, the direct video pipe opens at 22:47:45.456. At close (22:48:13.540), `lastWriteType=11` and `writeIdleMs=13402` place the last completed video write at approximately 22:48:00.138. Touch input continues until 22:48:05.102, about five seconds later. There is no direct audio stream in this session. That strongly points to the video channel becoming idle before the HU stops talking; it does not establish the HU's internal timeout rule.

In the 22:52:35 export, direct video starts at 22:52:04.884; music starts at 22:52:05.438. Source PCM is consumed at approximately 48,000 frames/s, `overwrittenFrames=0`, audio USB `dropped=0`, and audio writes continue until close. Last HU GPS arrives at 22:52:22.418, with close at 22:52:31.177. Audio traffic therefore does not prove video-channel liveness. Previous logs only printed video counters once per 150 frames, so they cannot give the last video-frame time in this session.

`0x00018010` is HU GPS, not a protocol heartbeat acknowledgement. The existing liveness detector learns its observed periodic cadence; it does not send or receive a new command-channel ping.

## Protocol reference and missing behavior

The Baidu-derived open protocol implementation declares `MSG_VIDEO_DATA=0x00020001`, `MSG_VIDEO_HEARTBEAT=0x00020002`, and a 12-byte video header. Its parser recognizes a separate heartbeat callback:

- https://github.com/Doris-duo/CarLife-MD-Lib/blob/main/LibSource/include/CTranRecvPackageProcess.h
- https://github.com/Doris-duo/CarLife-MD-Lib/blob/main/LibSource/core/CTranRecvPackageProcess.cpp

CarProjection previously sent video data only. Android screen capture commonly produces frequent pictures, including the local cursor overlay. CarPlay may produce no new pictures while its screen is unchanged; moving the Android cursor does not change the directly forwarded iPhone picture. A persistent video pipe alone sends no CarLife traffic.

## Change

After HU video START and active projection permission, send an empty video heartbeat when neither a video picture nor a heartbeat has completed a USB write for one second. The packet is a VIDEO AOA envelope containing a 12-byte video header with zero body length, current timestamp and service `0x00020002`. No H.264 picture is replayed or fabricated.

Scheduling and writes use the existing serialized USB writer. Check the current USB generation and projection state before writing. The clock is refreshed only after a real successful video-channel write, never by audio or touch. Normal video suppresses unnecessary heartbeats. Disconnect/projection stop prevent further heartbeat sends.

Every two seconds, `[VIDEO-FLOW]` records actual completed video frame/byte counts, picture idle time, successfully written heartbeat count, HU input idle time and queue drops. Close logs include these final video measurements. These counters also cover projection fallback.

The learned HU silence detector remains in place. This release does not claim that absent video keepalive is the only possible cause, nor that USB writes prove HU decoding. Profile/level rejection, phone background suspension, or a real cable problem remain distinguishable follow-up paths if heartbeats fail to keep this HU connected.

## Verification and physical test

The local JVM suite checks static and continuous video timing, reconnect isolation, the empty AOA/video packet layout, network byte order and timestamp wrap. Android build/lint run in GitHub Actions.

Only update CarProjection to 0.4.3; keep DiPlay carLife.3. Leave CarPlay on an unchanged screen for 60 seconds, then move the touchpad without selecting an item, then play music and swipe pages for two minutes. Export CarProjection logs if the HU still exits. `heartbeat USB sent` plus rising `heartbeats` with increasing `frameIdleMs` is the intended static-screen behavior. If the HU still stops input while these counters advance, missing video heartbeat alone is insufficient and the new logs should preserve the timing.
