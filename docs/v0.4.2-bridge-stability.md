# 0.4.2 — bridge stability after Lexus test

Paired DiPlay: 0.2.9-carLife.3-hud-test. Update both APKs.

The supplied audio-only log starts a media pipe at 22:04:52.072 and shows roughly
44KB/s mono TTS output instead of the nominal 96KB/s at 48k. HU periodic status
stops at 22:05:06 while phone output continues, and no native USB error arrives.
The supplied video log shows three video pipes reopening at 22:07:06.823,
22:07:06.919 and 22:07:06.985. An older persisted EIO reason in its header is not
proof of a new USB failure in this session.

Changes:
- Repeated identical avcC/SPS/PPS does not close the DiPlay pipe. This prevents
  open -> forceKeyFrame -> repeated config -> close -> open recovery loops.
- Decoded PCM no longer overwrites older queued source samples when ahead of
  the car clock. The pipe reader waits for bounded mixer space; the DiPlay
  decoder worker uses bounded queue admission and restores local playback if
  its consumer stalls for 250ms. The UI/network reader does not perform pipe IO.
- Wireless music uses the upstream DiPlay buffer plan (300ms start, 500ms
  capacity), voice uses 20ms start / 120ms capacity. Small source gaps keep
  20ms PCM output paced with silence, without consuming incomplete AAC tails.
- Logs include source/consumed/buffered/overwritten frame counts. USB sent output
  counts alone include short-gap padding and cannot establish source continuity.
- After at least three timely periodic HU 0x18010 messages, eight seconds with
  no HU input closes the stale transport and updates phone UI. Head units that
  do not demonstrate periodic messages are not timed out solely for inactivity.

The input logs expose these software defects but do not prove the ultimate HU
session termination cause. Retest music alone then direct video + music for
at least two minutes; export both CarProjection and DiPlay logs if it fails.
Car visible cursor/HID remains outside this patch. Actual vehicle timing and
radio conditions require another physical test.
